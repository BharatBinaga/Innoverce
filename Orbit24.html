      FAQ

      Before you launch

      Who can take part?

      Any current college student. Mix colleges and years freely. Teams are 1 to 4 people.

      Can I start building before the event?

      You can research and sketch ideas. Code must start at T+0, and judges may check your repository history.

      What should I bring?

      A laptop, charger, college ID and anything you want to hack on, such as boards or sensors. Food and drinks are provided.

      Do I need to pick a track now?

      No. You can change tracks until the problem statements are released at the opening session.
